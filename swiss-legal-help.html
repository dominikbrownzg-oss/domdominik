Where to get real help

  • Free initial legal advice: most cantonal bar associations run low-cost or free consultation sessions (search “permanence juridique” + your canton).
  • Tenants: tenants’ associations such as ASLOCA / Mieterverband.
  • Employees: trade unions, and your cantonal labour office.
  • Legal aid (assistance judiciaire): if you can’t afford a lawyer or court costs, apply at your cantonal court.
  • Victims of crime: cantonal victim support centres (LAVI / OHG). Emergencies: 117 police, 144 ambulance.
  • Official laws: fedlex.admin.ch (federal law, all languages).
